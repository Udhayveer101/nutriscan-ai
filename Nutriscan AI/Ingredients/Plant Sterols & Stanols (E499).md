## Regulatory status
EU mandatory warnings (Reg. 1169/2011 Annex III, formerly 608/2004); EFSA-authorised LDL claim (1.5–3 g/day)

## Assessment
Plant compounds that block cholesterol absorption; 1.5–3 g/day lowers LDL by about 7–12%. EU labels must warn that they are not for people who don't need to lower cholesterol, nor for pregnant or nursing women or under-5s, because they also reduce carotenoid absorption.

## Population-specific concerns
- pregnant or breastfeeding women
- children under 5
- sitosterolaemia
