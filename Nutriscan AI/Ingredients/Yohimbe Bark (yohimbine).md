# Yohimbe Bark (yohimbine)

**Category:** stimulant  ·  **Function:** Bark extract sold in libido and fat-burner supplements
**Evidence strength:** MODERATE

## Regulatory status
Prohibited in food in the EU (Reg. 1925/2006 Annex III Part A, added by Reg. (EU) 2019/650)

## Assessment
Yohimbine raises blood pressure and heart rate and can cause anxiety and arrhythmias. EFSA could not establish a safe intake and found supplement labels poorly reflect actual yohimbine content; the EU then banned yohimbe bark and its preparations in food.

## Population-specific concerns
- everyone
- people with heart disease, anxiety or high blood pressure
