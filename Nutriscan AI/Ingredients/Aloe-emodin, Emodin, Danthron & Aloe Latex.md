# Aloe-emodin, Emodin, Danthron & Aloe Latex

**Category:** processing aid  ·  **Function:** Stimulant-laxative anthranoids in aloe latex and some 'detox' products
**Evidence strength:** MODERATE

| Position | Concern |

## Regulatory status
Prohibited in food in the EU (Reg. 1925/2006 Annex III Part A, added by Reg. (EU) 2021/468); danthron IARC Group 2B

## Assessment
Hydroxyanthracene derivatives from the yellow latex under aloe leaf skin and some other plants. EFSA found aloe-emodin, emodin and danthron genotoxic and aloe extracts carcinogenic in rats, and could not set a safe intake. Decolourised inner-leaf aloe vera gel is a different product.

## Population-specific concerns
- everyone
- pregnancy
- children
