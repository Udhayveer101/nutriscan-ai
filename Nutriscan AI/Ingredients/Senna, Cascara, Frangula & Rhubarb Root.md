# Senna, Cascara, Frangula & Rhubarb Root

**Category:** processing aid  ·  **Function:** Stimulant-laxative herbs in 'detox', 'slimming' and laxative teas

## Regulatory status
Under EU scrutiny in food (Reg. 1925/2006 Annex III Part C, added by Reg. (EU) 2021/468); authorised only as short-term herbal medicines

## Assessment
Herbs whose hydroxyanthracene glycosides (such as sennosides) irritate the bowel. Fine as a short medicinal course, but daily 'detox' tea use causes diarrhoea, potassium loss and dependence. EFSA could not set a safe intake because some anthranoids are genotoxic.

## Population-specific concerns
- pregnancy
- children
- people with bowel disease
