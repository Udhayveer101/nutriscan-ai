## Regulatory status
EU Reg. 1334/2008 (every flavouring substance on the Union list); FDA 21 CFR 101.22 / 172.515; JECFA evaluates individual substances

## Assessment
A blend of aroma chemicals used in tiny amounts, so the toxicological risk is low: each substance is evaluated individually, and FDA delisted 7 synthetic flavours in 2018. The real issues are opacity (the label never says which chemicals, or whether they are animal-derived) and that flavourings are a NOVA marker of ultra-processed food.

## Population-specific concerns
- vegetarians/vegans (natural flavours may be animal-derived)
- people with rare flavour sensitivities
