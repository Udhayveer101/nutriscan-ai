## Regulatory status
FDA GRAS (21 CFR 184.1097); EU-authorised wine/beer processing aid

## Assessment
Plant polyphenols that bind proteins, so they are used to clear haze from drinks and add astringency. At very high intake they reduce iron absorption and irritate the gut, but use levels are small and much of the tannin is removed with the haze.
