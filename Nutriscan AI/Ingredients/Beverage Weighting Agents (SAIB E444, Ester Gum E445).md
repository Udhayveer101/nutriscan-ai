## Regulatory status
EU/FDA approved; the usual replacements for banned brominated vegetable oil

## Assessment
Used at low levels in cloudy citrus sodas to stop flavour oils floating. EFSA's re-evaluations found no safety concern; a clear improvement over BVO.
