## Regulatory status
FDA recognises resistant maltodextrin/dextrin as dietary fibre (2018); EU food ingredient

## Assessment
Starch processed so most of it resists digestion and is fermented in the colon like other soluble fibres. Used to add fibre and replace sugar; large servings can cause gas.

## Population-specific concerns
- IBS (bloating at high intake)
