# Coumarin

**Category:** flavour  ·  **Function:** Sweet hay/vanilla-like flavour constituent (cassia cinnamon, tonka)
**Evidence strength:** STRONG

**ADI:** TDI 0.1 mg/kg bw/day (EFSA 2008)

## Regulatory status
May not be added as such in the EU; maximum levels in cinnamon-containing foods (e.g. 50 mg/kg traditional bakery); banned as a direct additive in the US (21 CFR 189.130)

## Assessment
Naturally present in cassia cinnamon and tonka beans. In a sensitive minority it damages the liver. EFSA's TDI of 0.1 mg/kg bw can be exceeded by children eating a lot of cassia-cinnamon foods. Ceylon cinnamon has little coumarin.

## Population-specific concerns
- children
- people with liver disease
