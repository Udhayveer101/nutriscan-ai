## Regulatory status
Permitted in Australia/NZ, China, Mexico and some others; not authorised in the EU or US (FDA petition never approved)

## Assessment
A dipeptide intense sweetener (aspartic acid + alanine amide). JECFA set an ADI, but few regulators approved it and long-term human data are sparse. It does not contain phenylalanine.
