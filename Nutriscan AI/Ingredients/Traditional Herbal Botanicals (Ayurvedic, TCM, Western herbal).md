# Traditional Herbal Botanicals (Ayurvedic, TCM, Western herbal)

## Regulatory status
Botanicals in food supplements (EU national rules; FSSAI nutraceutical schedule; US DSHEA); some have EMA traditional-use monographs

## Assessment
Herbs used in Ayurvedic, Chinese and Western herbal traditions. At food or tea amounts they are generally safe; benefits of concentrated extracts are mostly unproven, and quality varies (adulteration and heavy-metal contamination have been found in some products).

## Population-specific concerns
- pregnancy and breastfeeding
- people on regular medication (interaction risk)

## Reference
EMA HMPC herbal monographs; Ayurvedic Pharmacopoeia of India; Saper et al. 2008, JAMA 300:915
