## Assessment
A very potent aspartame-derived sweetener, used in tiny amounts and considered safe within the ADI. A 2024 in-vitro study reported gut-epithelial and microbiome damage; this is early laboratory evidence, not a regulatory conclusion. Unlike aspartame it carries no phenylalanine warning.

## Population-specific concerns
