## Regulatory status
Approved EU (≤250 mg/L) & FDA; hydrolyses to methanol + CO₂ within hours

## Assessment
Added to beverages to kill yeasts/moulds, then breaks down almost entirely into methanol and CO₂ before the drink is consumed. Methanol contribution is far below that of many fruit juices. EFSA re-evaluation (2015) raised no safety concern at permitted use.
