# Red Yeast Rice / Monascus (monacolins, angkak)

**Category:** coloring  ·  **Function:** Traditional red colour; cholesterol supplement (monacolin K)
**Evidence strength:** MODERATE

## Regulatory status
EU limits monacolins to <3 mg per daily portion with mandatory warnings (Reg. (EU) 2022/860); not an authorised EU colour

## Assessment
Rice fermented with Monascus mould. It colours Chinese foods red and is sold as a 'natural statin' because monacolin K is identical to lovastatin. EFSA could not identify a safe intake: muscle and liver side effects were reported even at 3 mg/day, and the mycotoxin citrinin can be present.

## Population-specific concerns
- pregnancy and breastfeeding
- people taking statins
- children
- adults over 70
