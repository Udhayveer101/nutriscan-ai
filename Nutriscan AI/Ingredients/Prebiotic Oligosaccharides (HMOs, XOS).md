# Prebiotic Oligosaccharides (HMOs, XOS)

**Category:** nutrient  ·  **Function:** Prebiotic carbohydrates for infant formula and supplements

## Regulatory status
Authorised novel foods in the EU with conditions of use (infant formula, supplements, some foods)

## Assessment
Short-chain carbohydrates that resist digestion and feed gut bacteria. Human milk oligosaccharides (HMOs) such as 2'-FL are made by fermentation to mirror breast milk; EFSA found them safe at breast-milk-like levels. XOS come from corn cobs or birch. High intakes can cause wind.



## Reference
EFSA NDA Panel 2015, safety of 2'-O-fucosyllactose and lacto-N-neotetraose as novel food ingredients; Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/2470
