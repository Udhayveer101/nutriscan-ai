# Melatonin (added to food or drink)

**Category:** supplement  ·  **Function:** Sleep hormone added to 'relaxation' foods and drinks

## Regulatory status
FDA: not approved, GRAS or prior-sanctioned for conventional foods (warning letters 2010–2015); permitted only in dietary supplements. EU: supplement health claims only (0.5–1 mg)

## Assessment
A hormone, not a food ingredient. In a brownie or drink the dose is unlabelled and easy to over-consume, causing daytime drowsiness; FDA treats it as an unapproved additive in conventional foods.

## Population-specific concerns
- children
- pregnancy
- people driving or operating machinery
- people on sedatives or anticoagulants
