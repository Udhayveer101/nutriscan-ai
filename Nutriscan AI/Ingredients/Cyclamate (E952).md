## Assessment
Banned by the FDA in 1969 after a bladder-tumour signal in rats; later reviews did not confirm human carcinogenicity and it remains approved in the EU within the ADI. The US ban persists. Metabolised by gut bacteria to cyclohexylamine in some people.
