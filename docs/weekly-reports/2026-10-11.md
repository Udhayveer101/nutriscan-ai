## 2. Ingredients added

**KB profiles:** 203 → 212 (9 new; 14 existing families extended). **Seed rows:** +76. The local seed went from 382 to 458 ingredients.

### New KB profiles
- **Polyoxyethylene (40) stearate, E431:** JECFA ADI 0–25 mg/kg (1973). EU-approved for wine only; EFSA hasn't re-evaluated it. MEDIUM, in line with the polysorbates
- **Thermally oxidised soya bean oil with mono-/diglycerides (TOSOM), E479b:** EFSA 2018 found too little ADME and genotoxicity data to confirm the SCF (25) or JECFA (30 mg/kg) ADI. EU-approved only in frying fat emulsions
- **Hydrogenated poly-1-decene, E907:** glazing agent. EFSA 2020 set an ADI of 20 mg/kg; no concern
- **Carbamide (urea), E927b:** sugar-free chewing gum only (30 g/kg). JECFA 1993 found it acceptable at up to 3%; EFSA accepted a dental claim
- **Food-grade mineral oil (E905a / liquid paraffin / petrolatum):** HIGH/MEDIUM/MEDIUM
  - It isn't an EU additive. FDA allows it under 21 CFR 172.878.
  - EFSA's 2023 opinion found MOAH a health concern.
  - JECFA allows 0–20 mg/kg for high-viscosity grades only.
  - `E905a` now resolves here; `E905` stays on glazing waxes.
- **Ethyl acetate:** a decaffeination and extraction solvent (Dir. 2009/32/EC, all uses) and a flavouring; JECFA ADI 0–25 mg/kg
- **Added caffeine (incl. guarana, kola nut):** MEDIUM when dominant or moderate, LOW at trace
  - Based on EFSA 2015: 400 mg/day for adults, 200 mg/day in pregnancy, and about 3 mg/kg/day as a guide for children.
  - **Bug fix:** "guarana" used to resolve to *guar gum* through the substring "guar".
- **Taurine & glucuronolactone:** EFSA 2009 found no concern at energy-drink levels
- **Protein isolates & textured proteins:** soy/pea/whey/milk protein isolates and concentrates, TVP, and hydrolysed collagen
  - LOW concern, but it counts as an industrial ingredient for NOVA (Monteiro 2019).
  - The existing "Whey Protein" and "Soy Protein" seed rows are unchanged; only their isolate aliases now resolve here.

### Families extended (one profile per family)

## 6. Open bugs and proposals (not changed unattended)

- **`/auth/error` (auth, proposal):** it echoes the raw `?error=` value and names server environment variables. Show friendly copy and log the code on the server instead.
- **`/onboarding` renders without a session** (auth; proposal).
- `/og-image.png` is still missing, so social previews are broken.
- **Caffeine, ethyl acetate and guarana show "Flavor Enhancer" as their DB category.** There's no stimulant or solvent category, and adding one is a seed/category design change. Proposal: add "Stimulant" and "Processing Aid" categories.
- **Vanaspati** (Indian hydrogenated vegetable fat) is unscored.
  - Older vanaspati was a partially hydrogenated oil.
  - FSSAI has capped trans fat at 2% since 2022, so it no longer belongs with PHO.
  - It needs a methodology decision: interesterified, fully hydrogenated, or its own profile.
- Honey is still in `WHOLEFOOD_KEYWORDS`, though WHO counts it as a free sugar. This needs a methodology decision.
- "Baking powder" stays unscored on purpose: its acid component can be an aluminium phosphate, so mapping it to the bicarbonate family would understate concern.
- **`check-kb` warnings** (E500/E501 sub-codes, sorbitol syrup) are still open. The fix is subcode-aware matching.
- **`npm run lint`** needs an ESLint flat config.
