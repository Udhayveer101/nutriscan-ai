## Regulatory status
EU requires 'contains liquorice' labelling, plus a warning for people with hypertension at ≥4 g/kg in confectionery or ≥50 mg/L in drinks (Reg. 1169/2011 Annex III)

## Assessment
Glycyrrhizin in liquorice root blocks an enzyme that inactivates cortisol in the kidney. Regular intakes above about 100 mg a day can raise blood pressure and lower potassium. Liquorice sweets and some herbal teas can supply this much.

## Population-specific concerns
- hypertension
- pregnancy
- heart or kidney disease
- people on diuretics or digoxin
