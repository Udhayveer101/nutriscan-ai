## Regulatory status
Food ingredients, not additives; FDA counts mixed plant cell-wall fibres as dietary fibre (2018)

## Assessment
Milled cell-wall material from fruit, cereal or vegetable by-products. Used to bind water, replace fat or add fibre. Mostly insoluble fibre, except cereal beta-glucan, a soluble fibre with an EU-authorised cholesterol-lowering claim at 3 g/day. No safety concern at use levels.



## Reference
