## Regulatory status
Amino acids permitted in foods for specific groups and supplements; nucleotides permitted in infant formula (Reg. (EU) 2016/127); no ULs set for most amino acids (IOM 2005)

## Assessment
Isolated amino acids and nucleotides used in sports drinks, protein products, supplements and infant formula. They are normal food constituents. No upper limits are set for most; single amino acids at very high doses can unbalance metabolism. A 1989 epidemic of eosinophilia-myalgia was traced to a contaminated batch of L-tryptophan, not to tryptophan itself.

## Population-specific concerns
- phenylketonuria (phenylalanine)
- people on MAO inhibitors or serotonergic drugs (tryptophan)
