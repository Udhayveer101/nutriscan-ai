## 2. Ingredients added

There are 36 new seed rows. Each resolves to a KB profile by name, by code and by every alias; I checked this with a script.

**New KB profiles (7):**

| Profile | Codes | Seed rows | Why / basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glycyrrhizin / liquorice extract | INS/E958 | Glycyrrhizin | SCF 2003 sets a 100 mg/day upper limit. EU "contains liquorice" labelling is in Reg. 1169/2011 Annex III. Concern is MEDIUM when dominant (blood pressure, low potassium) |
| Dehydroacetic acid & sodium salt | INS 265/266 | Dehydroacetic Acid, Sodium Dehydroacetate | FDA allows it only on cut squash at ≤65 ppm (21 CFR 172.130), and the EU doesn't authorise it. **It was previously mis-scored as vinegar** (it matched "acetic acid") |
| Curdlan | INS 424 | Curdlan | JECFA ADI not specified (2001); 21 CFR 172.809 |
| Tannic acid | INS 181 | Tannic Acid | 21 CFR 184.1097; JECFA ADI not specified (1989) |
| Alitame | INS 956 | Alitame | JECFA ADI 0–1 mg/kg. Approved in AU/NZ, CN and MX; not approved in the EU or US |
| Sodium lauryl sulfate | INS 487 | Sodium Lauryl Sulfate | 21 CFR 172.822: dried egg whites ≤1000 ppm, marshmallow ≤0.5% of gelatin. Not authorised in the EU |
| Free-form amino acids (BCAAs, lysine…) | INS 641/642 | L-Leucine, L-Lysine HCl | Nutrient/flavouring status. VKM could not set safe doses for BCAA supplements |

**Rows added to existing families (29), with no new profile:**
- **Fast Green FCF (INS 143)** joins the FD&C dye group, which already owned "green 3". Source: JECFA 2017 ADI 0–25 mg/kg.
- **Erythorbates:** potassium (INS 317) and calcium (INS 318).
- **Phosphates:** ammonium phosphates (INS 342) go to the phosphate family; edible bone phosphate (INS 542) goes to calcium phosphates.
- **Acid salts:** magnesium lactate (INS 329) goes to lactates; magnesium citrate (INS 345) to citrates; sodium fumarate (INS 365, plus 366–368 as codes) to fumarates. Ammonium malate (INS 349) and ammonium acetate (INS 264) were added as codes or aliases.
- **Emulsifiers:** monoacetin (INS 1516) goes to the triacetin family, sorbitan tripalmitate (INS 496) to sorbitan esters, and polyoxyethylene (8) stearate (INS 430) to the POE-stearate family.
- **Glazing waxes:** rice bran wax (INS 908) and lanolin (INS 913).
- **Silicates:** sodium (INS 550), potassium (INS 560) and zinc (INS 557).
- **Modified starch:** distarch glycerol (INS 1411). It was **previously mis-scored as glycerol**.
- **Flour treatment:** potassium and ammonium persulphates (INS 922/923); also "chlorinated flour" and E925.
- **Plant colours:** saffron (INS 164, "kesar", crocin).
- **Sulphate salts:** magnesium sulphate (INS 518, "epsom salt"). "Epsom salt" was **previously treated as a whole food** because it matched "salt".
- **Minerals:** magnesium gluconate (INS 580), copper sulphate (INS 519), ferrous carbonate (INS 505) and calcium glycerophosphate (INS 383).
- **Vitamins:** nicotinic acid (INS 375).

**Aliases and codes added to existing profiles:**
- Disodium EDTA picks up INS 386.
- Gelatin picks up E441, matching its seed row. Before this, "gelatine (E441)" didn't resolve.
- Ethyl acetate picks up INS 1504.
- "Sugar alcohol" and "polyols" now go to polyols. Before, "sugar alcohol" was **hijacked by "sugar" → sucrose (HIGH)**.

KB totals: 212 → 219 profiles, 957 → 1054 keywords, 358 → 402 E/INS codes. The seed grew from 458 to 494 rows.

**Regression check:** I resolved all 1,802 seed names, aliases and codes against the old and new KB. One unintended change turned up: "ficin" matched inside "Zingiber off**icin**ale" (ginger). I removed the keyword. Every other change is a new term or one of the deliberate fixes above.

## 6. Open bugs and proposals (not changed unattended)

- **`/auth/error` (auth):** it echoes the raw `?error=` value.
- **`/onboarding` renders without a session (auth).**

## 7. Ideas for next week

1. **Liquorice and blood-pressure personalisation.** If the user profile has hypertension or pregnancy, raise glycyrrhizin to HIGH and show the SCF 100 mg/day limit. The same pattern would give per-user caffeine budgets.
2. **INS-first display for Indian labels.** FSSAI labels use "INS 958" style codes. Show the INS code alongside the E-number on ingredient pages, and accept "acidity regulator (INS 330)" in search. The API already normalises "INS 211" to E211.
3. **"Not EU-authorised" badge.** Many profiles now carry "not authorised in the EU" (Fast Green, SLS, dehydroacetate, persulphates, alitame). A badge on results would be a clear, factual cross-jurisdiction signal.
4. **Seed-alias coverage gate.** Add the check I scripted this week to `check-kb.ts`: every seed alias must resolve to the same profile as its row's name. It would have flagged the "ficin in officinale" hijack.
5. **Exact-match search ranking:** an exact name, alias or code hit should outrank `contains` matches (carried over).
