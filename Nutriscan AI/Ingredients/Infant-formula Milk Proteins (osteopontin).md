# Infant-formula Milk Proteins (osteopontin)

**Category:** nutrient  ·  **Function:** Milk glycoprotein added to infant formula to match breast milk

## Regulatory status
EFSA: safe up to 151 mg/L in infant and follow-on formula (2022), EU novel food; FDA GRAS notice GRN 716 (no questions)

## Assessment
A protein abundant in human breast milk but low in cow's milk, isolated from whey and added to some premium formulas. EFSA found it safe at the proposed levels, though claimed immune benefits are not established.

## Population-specific concerns
- milk allergy (it is a cow's-milk protein)
