## Regulatory status
EU wine processing aid (fungal chitosan); EFSA-backed claim for maintaining normal LDL cholesterol at 3 g/day

## Assessment
A fibre made from crustacean shells or fungal cell walls. It clears wine and juice and, in supplements at 3 g/day, helps maintain normal LDL cholesterol. It can bind fat-soluble vitamins at high intake.

## Population-specific concerns
- crustacean allergy (shellfish-derived chitosan)
