## Regulatory status
Food ingredient (FDA standard of identity for lemon juice, 21 CFR 146.114)

## Assessment
Used for sourness, not sweetness: lemon juice has about 2.5 g sugar per 100 g against roughly 10 g for apple juice, so the concentrate adds citric acid rather than meaningful free sugar. Very frequent acidic foods can erode tooth enamel.
