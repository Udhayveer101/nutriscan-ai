## Regulatory status
EU flavouring; Reg. 1169/2011 Annex III requires 'contains liquorice' and a hypertension warning at ≥4 g/kg (confectionery) or ≥50 mg/l (drinks); FDA GRAS as a flavour

## Assessment
Glycyrrhizin blocks cortisol breakdown, causing sodium retention and potassium loss. Regular intake above about 100 mg/day can raise blood pressure and, in heavy liquorice eaters, cause dangerously low potassium. Trace use as a flavour is not a concern.

## Population-specific concerns
- hypertension
- heart or kidney disease
- pregnancy
- people taking diuretics or digoxin
