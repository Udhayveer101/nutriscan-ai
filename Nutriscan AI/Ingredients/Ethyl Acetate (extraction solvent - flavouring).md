## Regulatory status
EU extraction solvent for all uses under good manufacturing practice (Dir. 2009/32/EC); FDA GRAS

## Assessment
A simple ester found naturally in fruit and wine. It is used to decaffeinate coffee and tea and as a carrier for flavours, and it evaporates during processing. No concern at the residue levels left in food.
