EU novel food (Decision 2005/457/EC; extended 2024); FDA GRAS

## Assessment
A disaccharide made enzymatically from sucrose. It is a sugar (4 kcal/g) and counts toward total sugars, but it is digested slowly (low GI) and is not cariogenic. Not the same as isomalt (E953), a polyol.
