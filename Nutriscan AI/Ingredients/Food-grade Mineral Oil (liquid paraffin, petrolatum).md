## Regulatory status
Not authorised as an additive in the EU; FDA permits food-grade white mineral oil (21 CFR 172.878) as a release agent and coating

## Assessment
Petroleum-derived hydrocarbons used to keep sweets, dried fruit and bakery goods from sticking. Saturated hydrocarbons (MOSH) build up in body tissues. EFSA's 2023 opinion found the aromatic fraction (MOAH) a health concern because it includes genotoxic carcinogens. Food-grade white oils are refined to remove most MOAH.
