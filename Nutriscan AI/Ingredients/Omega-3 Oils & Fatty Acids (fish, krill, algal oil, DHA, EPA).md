## Regulatory status
EU-authorised claims for EPA+DHA and heart function (250 mg/day); EFSA: supplemental EPA+DHA up to 5 g/day raises no safety concern

## Assessment
Oils rich in EPA and DHA, the long-chain omega-3 fats found in oily fish. They support normal heart, brain and vision function. EFSA found no safety concern up to 5 g/day of supplemental EPA+DHA. Cod liver oil also contains preformed vitamin A, which is limited in pregnancy.

## Population-specific concerns
- fish or crustacean allergy (source-dependent)
- pregnancy (cod liver oil: vitamin A content)
- people on anticoagulants at high doses
