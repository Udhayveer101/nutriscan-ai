## Regulatory status
Prohibited in food in the EU (Reg. 1925/2006 Annex III Part A, added by Reg. (EU) 2015/403); dietary supplements containing ephedrine alkaloids banned by the FDA (2004)

## Assessment
Ephedra herb contains ephedrine, an amphetamine-like stimulant. Supplements were linked to strokes, heart attacks, arrhythmias and deaths, which led the FDA to ban them in 2004. The EU prohibits the herb and its preparations in food.

## Population-specific concerns
- everyone
- people with heart disease or high blood pressure
