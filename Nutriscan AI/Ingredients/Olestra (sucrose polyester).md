## Regulatory status
FDA-approved for savoury snacks (21 CFR 172.867); foods must be fortified with vitamins A, D, E and K; not authorised in the EU or Canada

## Assessment
Sucrose esterified with 6 to 8 fatty acids, too big to digest, so it adds no calories. It can cause cramping and loose stools, and it carries fat-soluble vitamins and carotenoids out of the gut, which is why the FDA requires added vitamins A, D, E and K. The on-pack warning was dropped in 2003.

## Population-specific concerns
- children
- people with bowel disorders
