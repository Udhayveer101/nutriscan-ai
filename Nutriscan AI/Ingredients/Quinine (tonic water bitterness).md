# Quinine (tonic water bitterness)

## Regulatory status
FDA ≤83 ppm in carbonated drinks (21 CFR 172.575); EU requires 'contains quinine' labelling (Directive 2002/67/EC)

## Assessment
The bitter alkaloid of cinchona bark. Tonic water contains far less than antimalarial doses, but rare hypersensitivity reactions (including blood disorders) have been reported even from tonic water, which is why its presence must be declared.

## Population-specific concerns
- pregnancy
- G6PD deficiency
- people with heart-rhythm disorders or on QT-prolonging drugs
- quinine hypersensitivity
