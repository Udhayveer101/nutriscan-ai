# Antimicrobial Wash & Sterilant Agents (peroxide, ozone, hypochlorite, peracetic acid)

**Category:** preservative  ·  **Function:** Processing aid: disinfects produce, carcasses, packaging or milk

## Regulatory status
FDA permits specified uses (21 CFR 173.368 ozone, 184.1366 hydrogen peroxide, 173.315 wash chemicals); EU restricts pathogen-reduction treatments of meat

## Assessment
Oxidisers that kill microbes on food surfaces and packaging, then break down to water, oxygen, acetic acid or salt. The main residue question is chlorate and other by-products from chlorine washes, which EFSA linked to iodine-uptake inhibition at high chronic exposure, chiefly for infants.

## Population-specific concerns
- infants and young children (chlorate residues from chlorinated water)

## Reference
EFSA CONTAM Panel 2015, risks for public health related to the presence of chlorate in food; 21 CFR 173.368, 184.1366
