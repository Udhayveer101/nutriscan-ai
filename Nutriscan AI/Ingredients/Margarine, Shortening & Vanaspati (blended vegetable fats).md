## Regulatory status
Industrial trans fat capped at 2 g/100 g fat in the EU (Reg. (EU) 2019/649) and in India (FSSAI, 2022); PHOs no longer GRAS in the US

## Assessment
Vegetable oils made solid by blending with palm fats, interesterification or full hydrogenation, plus emulsifiers. Modern margarines and Indian vanaspati are capped at 2% trans fat, so the old trans-fat problem is largely solved where rules are enforced; they remain industrial (NOVA 4) fats, often high in saturated fat from palm.
