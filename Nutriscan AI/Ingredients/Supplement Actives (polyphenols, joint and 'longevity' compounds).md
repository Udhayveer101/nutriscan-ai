## Regulatory status
Food supplement ingredients; several are EU novel foods with intake limits; EFSA has rejected most health claims

## Assessment
Compounds sold for joints, skin, energy or 'anti-ageing'. Generally well tolerated at labelled doses, but most marketed benefits have not been substantiated by EFSA, and concentrated polyphenol extracts can interact with medicines.

## Population-specific concerns
- people on blood thinners (several polyphenol extracts)
- shellfish allergy (glucosamine)
