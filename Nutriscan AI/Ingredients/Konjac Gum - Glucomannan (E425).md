EU-approved but banned in jelly mini-cups (choking deaths); GRAS

## Assessment
A highly absorbent soluble fibre that forms firm gels. Safe to eat, but firm konjac jelly cups caused choking deaths, so the EU bans it in jelly confectionery.

## Population-specific concerns
