## Regulatory status
EU-approved only in chewing gum without added sugar (max 30 g/kg)

## Assessment
Urea is a natural body metabolite. In sugar-free gum it neutralises plaque acids. EFSA accepted a dental health claim for this effect (at least 20 mg per piece). No toxicological concern at this use.
