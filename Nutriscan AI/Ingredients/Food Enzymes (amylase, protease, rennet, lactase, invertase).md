Approved EU & FDA GRAS

## Assessment
Enzymes that improve dough handling, volume and shelf life, clot milk for cheese (rennet/chymosin), or break down lactose. Most are denatured by baking or stay in tiny amounts. Very low consumer concern; enzyme dust is a recognised occupational allergen for bakers.

## Population-specific concerns
- bakery workers (occupational allergen); rare food allergy
- vegetarians (animal rennet is from calf stomach; microbial rennet is not)
