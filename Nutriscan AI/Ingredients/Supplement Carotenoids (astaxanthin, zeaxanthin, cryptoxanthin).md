# Supplement Carotenoids (astaxanthin, zeaxanthin, cryptoxanthin)

**Category:** nutrient  ·  **Function:** Antioxidant pigment in supplements and fish feed

## Regulatory status
Authorised novel foods for supplements with maximum daily amounts (astaxanthin 8 mg/day, EFSA 2020)

## Assessment
Red and yellow carotenoid pigments. Astaxanthin gives salmon its colour (via feed) and is sold from Haematococcus algae; zeaxanthin accumulates in the retina. EFSA set safe supplement limits; claimed eye and skin benefits are not authorised.



## Reference
EFSA NDA Panel 2020, Safety of astaxanthin for its use as a novel food in food supplements
