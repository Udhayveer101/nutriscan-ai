## Regulatory status
FDA 'no questions' letters for brazzein-53 (GRN 1142, 2024) and brazzein-54 (2025); not authorised in the EU

## Assessment
A small protein from the West African oubli fruit that tastes about 1,000 times sweeter than sugar. It is now brewed by yeast and is digested like any other protein, so it adds no sugar. Long-term data are limited because it is new to food.
