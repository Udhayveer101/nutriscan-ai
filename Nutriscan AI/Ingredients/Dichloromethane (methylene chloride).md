## Regulatory status
EU residue limits 2 mg/kg roasted coffee, 5 mg/kg tea (Dir. 2009/32/EC); FDA 10 ppm in decaffeinated coffee (21 CFR 173.255); IARC Group 2A

## Assessment
A chlorinated solvent IARC classifies as probably carcinogenic to humans (Group 2A), mainly from occupational and animal data. Decaf residues are small and within legal limits, but because it is a carcinogen by classification and water or CO2 processes exist, it carries a residual concern. The US EPA banned most consumer and commercial uses in 2024.
