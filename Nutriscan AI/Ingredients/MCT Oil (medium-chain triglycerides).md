## Regulatory status
US: GRAS (Stepan affirmation petition GRASP 4G0409; caprylic acid 21 CFR 184.1025); used in EU foods for special medical purposes; ordinary food fat elsewhere

## Assessment
Fats made from the C8 and C10 fatty acids of coconut or palm kernel oil, absorbed straight to the liver and burned quickly. They are used in medical feeds for fat malabsorption. They are still saturated fat with 9 kcal/g, weight-loss claims are weak, and large doses cause stomach cramps and diarrhoea.
