## Regulatory status
FDA permits it in paprika/chilli powder (21 CFR 172.140); not an authorised food additive in the EU, where its feed authorisation was suspended in 2017

## Assessment
A synthetic antioxidant that keeps ground chilli and paprika red. The EU suspended it in animal feed (Reg. 2017/962) because EFSA could not rule out that p-phenetidine, a production impurity and possible mutagen, was present, and data gaps remained. Direct food exposure is small, but the safety dossier is incomplete.
