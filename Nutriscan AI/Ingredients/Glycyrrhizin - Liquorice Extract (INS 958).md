## Regulatory status
EU flavouring with mandatory 'contains liquorice' label at ≥100 mg/kg (hypertension warning at ≥4 g/kg in confectionery); FDA GRAS as a flavour

## Assessment
Glycyrrhizin is 50× sweeter than sugar. Regular intake above about 100 mg/day can raise blood pressure and lower potassium (pseudo-aldosteronism), which is why EU labels must say 'contains liquorice'. Harmless in the small amounts used as flavour.

## Population-specific concerns
- hypertension
- heart or kidney disease
- pregnancy
- people on diuretics or potassium-lowering drugs
