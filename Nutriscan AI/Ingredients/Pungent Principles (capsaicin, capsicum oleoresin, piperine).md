## Regulatory status
Capsaicin may not be added as such in the EU (Reg. 1334/2008 Annex III); chilli and pepper extracts used as flavourings

## Assessment
Concentrated chilli or pepper extracts. At culinary levels they are safe. Very concentrated capsaicin products have caused vomiting, chest pain and hospital visits in children, and the SCF could not set a safe upper intake. Piperine in supplements can increase absorption of some medicines.

## Population-specific concerns
- children (extreme-heat 'challenge' products)
- gastro-oesophageal reflux

SCF 2002 opinion on capsaicin; Regulation (EC) 1334/2008 Annex III
