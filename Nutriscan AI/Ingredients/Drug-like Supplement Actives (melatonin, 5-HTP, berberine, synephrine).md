## Regulatory status
Medicines or restricted in several countries (melatonin is prescription-only in the UK); sold as supplements in the US

## Assessment
Supplement ingredients with genuine drug-like effects: hormones, neurotransmitter precursors, stimulants and enzyme-inhibiting alkaloids. They can work, which is exactly why they carry side effects and interactions that ordinary food ingredients do not.

## Population-specific concerns
- people taking antidepressants (5-HTP)
- people on any regular medication (berberine)
- heart conditions (synephrine)
- pregnancy
- children
