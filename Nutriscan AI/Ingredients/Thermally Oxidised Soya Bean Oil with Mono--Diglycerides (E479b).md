## Assessment
A niche emulsifier that stops margarine-type frying fats spattering. EFSA's 2018 re-evaluation found no adverse effects up to the highest dose tested in rats, but too little absorption and genotoxicity data to confirm the old ADI. Exposure is very low because use is so narrow.
