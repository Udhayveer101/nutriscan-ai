## Regulatory status
Listed on the EU Union list of flavourings (Reg. (EU) 872/2012); evaluated by JECFA as flavouring agents; FDA permits under 21 CFR 172.515 or GRAS

## Assessment
Single aroma chemicals, from natural sources or synthesised, used to give mint, cinnamon, clove, citrus, almond, anise or wintergreen notes. At flavouring levels JECFA and EFSA found no safety concern. Several are skin sensitisers, and methyl salicylate (oil of wintergreen) is toxic to small children in concentrated form.

## Population-specific concerns
- salicylate-sensitive people and young children (methyl salicylate)
- skin-sensitised people (cinnamaldehyde, citral, eugenol)
