## Regulatory status
FDA permits only on the skins of oranges not intended for processing, ≤2 ppm (21 CFR 74.302); IARC Group 2B; not permitted in the EU

## Assessment
An azo dye sprayed on some Florida orange peels. IARC classes it as possibly carcinogenic (Group 2B) from bladder tumours in animals. Flesh exposure is minimal, but zesting or candying peel delivers it directly.
