## Regulatory status
Permitted in Australia/NZ, China and some other markets; not authorised in the EU or US

## Assessment
A dipeptide sweetener made from aspartic acid and alanine. JECFA set an ADI after animal studies showed liver weight increases at high doses. It is rarely used, and fewer regulators have reviewed it than aspartame or sucralose.
