## Regulatory status
IARC Group 1 carcinogen with or without tobacco (Vol. 85, 2004; Vol. 100E, 2012); tobacco and nicotine are prohibited as food ingredients in India (FSS Prohibition and Restrictions on Sales Regulations 2011, 2.3.4)

## Assessment
The seed of the areca palm, chewed alone, in paan or in pan masala and sweetened supari mixes. IARC classes areca nut itself, even without tobacco, as causing oral and oesophageal cancer; it also causes oral submucous fibrosis, a precancerous scarring of the mouth.
