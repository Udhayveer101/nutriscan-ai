# Herbs with Liver, Kidney or Interaction Risks (ashwagandha, kava, St John's wort…)

**Category:** nutrient  ·  **Function:** Herbal supplement ingredient

## Regulatory status
Restricted, banned or carrying mandatory warnings in some countries (kava, ashwagandha in Denmark, black cohosh liver warning)

## Assessment
Herbs with published cases of liver or kidney injury, or with strong medicine interactions (St John's wort makes the contraceptive pill and many drugs less effective). Occasional food-level exposure is not the concern; concentrated supplements are.

## Population-specific concerns
- liver disease
- pregnancy
- people on any regular medication
- people on blood thinners

## Reference
NIDDK LiverTox; WHO 2007 kava hepatotoxicity assessment; DTU Food 2020 ashwagandha risk assessment; IARC Vol. 108 (ginkgo)
