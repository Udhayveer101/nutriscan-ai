## Regulatory status
EU Directive 2009/32/EC sets permitted solvents and residue limits; FDA residue limits in 21 CFR 173 (e.g. 173.270 hexane, 173.210 acetone)

## Assessment
Solvents used to pull oil from seeds, decaffeinate coffee or make spice oleoresins. They are removed by evaporation and only trace residues within legal limits remain. Their hazards (hexane neurotoxicity, methanol toxicity) apply at occupational or poisoning doses, not food residues.
