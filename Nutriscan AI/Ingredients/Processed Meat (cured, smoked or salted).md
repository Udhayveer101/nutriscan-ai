## Regulatory status
Food; IARC Group 1 carcinogen (colorectal cancer)

## Assessment
Meat transformed by salting, curing, fermentation or smoking. IARC classifies processed meat as carcinogenic to humans (Group 1): each 50 g eaten daily raises colorectal cancer risk by about 18%. Usually also high in salt and often cured with nitrite.

## Population-specific concerns
- anyone eating it daily; colorectal cancer risk rises ~18% per 50 g/day
