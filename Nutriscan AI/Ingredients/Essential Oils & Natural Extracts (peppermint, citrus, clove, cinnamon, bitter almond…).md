## Regulatory status
Natural flavourings under Reg. (EC) 1334/2008; FDA GRAS (21 CFR 182.20); maximum levels apply to restricted constituents (pulegone, estragole, coumarin, HCN)

## Assessment
Concentrated aroma oils used in drops. At flavouring levels they are safe. Neat oils are potent: some carry restricted constituents (pulegone in mint, coumarin in cassia, hydrogen cyanide in unrectified bitter almond), and bergamot oil contains phototoxic furocoumarins. Dominant use (e.g. oil-based sweets) is unusual and merits caution.

## Population-specific concerns
- children (concentrated oils are toxic if swallowed neat)
- people with citrus or mint allergy
