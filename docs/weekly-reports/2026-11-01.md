E230–E233 were the main gap in E-number coverage. Every other EU-listed code already resolved. These fungicides are what UK and EU loose citrus labels mean by "waxed, treated with imazalil/thiabendazole". They're scored MEDIUM because residues sit on the peel, and IARC classes the sodium OPP salt as Group 2B.

### Aliases and codes added to existing entries
- **E121** → Citrus Red No. 2 (the row and profile had no code). Also the keyword "citrus red".
- **E161h** → Zeaxanthin (row and `supplement-carotenoids`).
- "tapioca syrup", "cassava syrup", "tapioca glucose syrup" → Glucose Syrup (a free sugar; these were unrecognised before).
- "acacia fibre", "acacia fiber", "acacia senegal gum" → Gum Arabic (E414).
- "acetone peroxide(s)" and E929 → flour bleaching agents.
- MCT synonyms: "medium(-)chain triglyceride(s)", "caprylic/capric triglyceride", "tricaprylin", "c8 mct", "mct powder".

### Lookup bug fixed
- "1,3-butanediol" / "butane-1,3-diol" used to resolve to **Packaging Gases**, because the bare "butane" keyword matched them. They now resolve to their own profile, since the longer keyword wins.
- "acetone peroxide" used to resolve to Extraction Solvents, via "acetone". It now resolves to flour bleaching agents.
