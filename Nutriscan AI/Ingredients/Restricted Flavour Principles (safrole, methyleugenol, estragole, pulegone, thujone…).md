# Restricted Flavour Principles (safrole, methyleugenol, estragole, pulegone, thujone…)

## Regulatory status
May not be added as such to food in the EU; maximum levels apply to foods containing flavourings or herbs (Reg. 1334/2008 Annex III); safrole and methyleugenol IARC Group 2B

## Assessment
Compounds found in basil, tarragon, fennel, nutmeg, sassafras, pennyroyal, sage and wormwood. Safrole, methyleugenol and estragole are genotoxic carcinogens in rodents; pulegone and menthofuran are liver-toxic; thujone affects the nervous system. Normal herb use gives low exposure; concentrated extracts and essential oils are the concern.

## Population-specific concerns
- children
- pregnancy
