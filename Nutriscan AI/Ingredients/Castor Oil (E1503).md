## Regulatory status
FDA food additive (21 CFR 172.876) as release agent; EU-listed as E1503 for limited uses

## Assessment
Used in tiny amounts so sweets and supplements don't stick. Its ricinoleic acid is a stimulant laxative at teaspoon doses, which is why the ADI is low. Refined castor oil contains no ricin.
