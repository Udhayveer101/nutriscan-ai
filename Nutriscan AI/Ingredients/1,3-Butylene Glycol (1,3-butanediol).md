## Regulatory status
FDA: flavouring solvent (21 CFR 173.220) and direct additive (21 CFR 172.712); not an authorised EU carrier additive

## Assessment
A glycol like propylene glycol that dissolves flavourings, used at the minimum amount needed. The body turns it into the ketone body beta-hydroxybutyrate, which is why it also appears in 'ketone' drinks. At those doses it can cause nausea and intoxication-like effects with alcohol.
