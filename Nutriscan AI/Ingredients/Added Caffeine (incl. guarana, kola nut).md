# Added Caffeine (incl. guarana, kola nut)

**Category:** stimulant  ·  **Function:** Stimulant / bitterness
**Evidence strength:** STRONG

**ADI:** No concern up to 400 mg/day (single dose 200 mg) for healthy adults; 200 mg/day in pregnancy (EFSA 2015)

| Position | Concern |

## Regulatory status
Permitted flavouring in soft drinks; EU requires a 'high caffeine content' warning above 150 mg/L

## Assessment
A stimulant added to colas and energy drinks, sometimes via guarana or kola nut. Adults are fine up to about 400 mg a day, but one energy drink can hold 80–160 mg, which is a lot for a child. EFSA linked energy-drink adverse events mainly to high caffeine intake, often with alcohol.

## Population-specific concerns
- children and adolescents (EFSA: about 3 mg/kg bw/day as a guide)
- pregnancy and breastfeeding (≤200 mg/day)
- people with heart-rhythm problems or anxiety

## Reference
EFSA NDA Panel 2015 Scientific Opinion on the safety of caffeine; EU Reg. 1169/2011 Annex III
